# Research Documentation

## AI Detection Limitations

AI detectors are not 100% accurate. 

**Example:**
- An AI-generated image may be detected as real.
- A real image may be detected as AI.

### False Positives
A false positive happens when the system misidentifies a genuine photo as AI-generated.

**Example:**
Real Image
↓
Detector says AI

This is called a False Positive.

### False Negatives
A false negative happens when the system fails to spot an AI image, labeling it as genuine.

**Example:**
AI Image
↓
Detector says Real

This is called a False Negative.

## Social Impact

The proliferation of AI-generated imagery has profound implications on society:

- **Deepfakes**: Highly realistic but fabricated media depicting people doing or saying things they didn't do.
- **Fake news**: Synthetic images created to lend false credibility to fabricated news stories.
- **Misinformation**: The unintentional or deliberate spread of false narratives through manipulated media.
- **Identity fraud**: Using AI to create fake personas or bypass identity verification systems.
- **Social media manipulation**: Employing synthetic images to influence trends and public discourse.

**Example:**
AI-generated images can be used to spread misinformation, damage reputations, and manipulate public opinion.
